# Support

Check the relevant repository's documentation, release notes, and open issues first. Include the repository, release or revision, and record identifier where applicable. Issues and pull requests in public repositories are public.

- For questions about a record, release, or index method, use the relevant index's issue tracker and follow its contribution guidance.
- For questions about shared policy, participation, or proposed domains, use the [organization issue tracker](https://github.com/software-commons/.github/issues).
- For a source-backed correction or source submission, follow the [contribution guide](https://github.com/software-commons/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- For conduct concerns, follow the reporting guidance in the [code of conduct](https://github.com/software-commons/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Direct questions about upstream standards, implementations, or downstream products to the respective publisher or project.
