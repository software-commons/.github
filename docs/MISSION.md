# Mission and scope

Software Commons is an independent initiative dedicated to the systematic discovery, synthesis, normalization, and standardization of distributed software engineering knowledge.

It develops focused, versioned, machine-readable reference indexes that reconcile fragmented technical information, preserve authoritative sources and meaningful distinctions, and provide consistent knowledge foundations for software development, research, interoperability, and automated reasoning. It operates independently of the software products and organizations that use its resources.

## Responsibilities

- Define the scope, identities, and normalized representations of the knowledge domains it indexes.
- Identify primary and authoritative sources for each kind of claim. Preserve source revisions, evidence, and the rationale for material normalization decisions.
- Publish versioned references and document validation methods, coverage limits, and unresolved conflicts.
- Standardize how each index represents in-scope knowledge. This standardization applies to the index and its published records.

## Scope boundaries

Software Commons is responsible for its synthesis and normalized representations within declared domains. Upstream standards and specifications, source documents, implementations, and experimental observations remain attributable to their respective publishers, projects, or investigators. A license or permission to access a source is evaluated separately from its authority as evidence.

Authoring external standards, certifying conformance, maintaining upstream implementations, and directing downstream product decisions are outside the initiative's scope. Validation tools and reproducibility materials may accompany index work when needed to support its evidence and publication.

See [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for decision authority and [index policy](INDEX_POLICY.md) for requirements on individual domains.
