# Code of conduct

Software Commons expects respectful participation in its GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, and other spaces it operates. These expectations apply to everyone participating or moderating.

## Participation

- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Discuss claims, methods, and evidence on their merits. Explain disagreements specifically and give others room to respond.
- Respect contributors' privacy and boundaries. Use information people have chosen to share in the relevant context.
- Accept good-faith corrections and give appropriate credit.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning behavior, targeted disruption, retaliation, and sharing private information without permission are prohibited.

## Reporting and moderation

Use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to report behavior or content on GitHub. If GitHub offers a report to repository administrators, that option can route repository conduct concerns to the people with moderation access. Keep sensitive reports and personal information out of public issues and pull requests.

People with repository moderation permissions may hide or remove content, restrict participation, or refer behavior to GitHub as appropriate to the harm, severity, and recurrence. Reports should be handled discreetly, with details shared only as needed to respond.
